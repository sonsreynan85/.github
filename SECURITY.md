# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

To privately report an exploitable security or privacy issue in this repository please [email the chairs](mailto:group-privacycg-chairs@w3.org) directly with information about the vulnerability.

The repository's editors will send a response indicating the next step![Uploading IMG_20250415_090201.jpg…]()
s in handling your report. After the initial reply to your report, the editors will keep you informed of the progress towards a fix and full announcement, and may ask for additional information or guidance.
